TilePilot template direction

Default Template Concepts

A practical template library should protect readable work areas, keep support windows visible, and avoid turning every display into a dense grid.

Most defaults should be work-shaped: one clear center of gravity, stable support slots, and predictable keyboard movement.
Primary Secondary Rail Context Overlay

Plain language

What are rails?

Rails are narrow support zones at the edge of a layout. They hold apps you glance at or touch briefly, not the main thing you are reading, writing, coding, or designing.
